# House rules overlay: Open Jar Games (kids' couch games). NOT for the adult track.

Applies only to Open Jar games (Kaden and his son play them together). When this process moves to
the game-studio profile, this file stays an Open Jar overlay; the AD role, templates and gates
carry over without it.

## Gameplay readability (all Open Jar games)
- No text in gameplay (menus may use text: they are for the adult).
- Bigger-than-me / food-vs-threat / interactable reads at a glance, at 1x.
- P1 green and P2 red belong to the player slot (rings, arrows, glows). Character colours keep a
  perceptual distance (ΔE threshold) from both.
- Nothing gory or frightening; no pain sounds; being hit or eaten is comic.
- Brand names become unbranded originals; risqué details become kid-safe.

## Look (per game, not global)
- The Jarling house style (round toy shapes, big faces and eyes, saturated colour, thick outlines)
  is the library look for 3D Jarling games. It does **not** drive a game's look when the owner
  says so: for Life of a Worm (Kaden, 2026-11-12) the look is designed "for an adult who loves
  beautifully made indie games, with his son as a co-player". A mascot grin or googly eye reads as
  kids' art there.
- No faces on vehicles or machines unless asked.

## Characters
- Library characters (Jarlings) are 3D-first: bible, 3D master on the Jarling kit, the owner's
  pick, then derived forms (pixel forms drawn over low-res renders, hand-cleaned at size). The 3D
  master is the master reference for those characters.
- Character sheets from the owner's son are credited as "Kaden's sheet <date>", never by who
  designed them.
- Read check for every character: 3D side view, pixel form at 1x and game scale, 16 px silhouette;
  nothing near a mouth may read as a pipe or cigarette.

## Privacy
- No personal info or real local places (house, shop, town) in any game or art, public or not.
